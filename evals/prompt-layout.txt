Report only defects in layout or in data. Examples of defects: one widget overlaps another widget, a widget is off the edge of the screen, text is clipped, a time or a date is not valid, a row is a duplicate, or a label has a typo that is clearly wrong.

Do not report a usual design choice. Do not report an alignment preference. Do not report a colour choice or a font choice.
